EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

1. Annual Leave Policy
Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Leave requests must be submitted to the manager at least five working
days before the planned leave date.

2. Working Hours
The standard working day is 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
Employees should inform their manager if they expect to arrive late.

3. Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.
Some roles may require employees to work from the office more frequently.

4. Expense Reimbursement
Employees must submit expense claims with valid receipts within 30 days
of the transaction. Approved claims are generally reimbursed in the
next monthly payroll cycle.
